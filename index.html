Write on the back of your photos.

PhotoBack turns every picture into a Polaroid-style card. Flip it over and write by hand: a date, a place, a name, a feeling. Later, search your own handwriting to find it again.

tap the photo to flip it ↻

A habit the digital age forgot

Every printed photo used to carry a note on the back. PhotoBack brings that back, for the photos already on your phone.

1.

Pick a photo

Choose one picture, a handful, or your whole library. You can also send one straight from the Photos app's share sheet.

2.

Flip it & write

Tap the card and it turns over to blank ruled paper. Write with your finger or Apple Pencil, or type if you prefer.

3.

Find it years later

PhotoBack reads your handwriting on your iPhone, so “that beach trip” brings the right card back.

the front stays untouched

A keepsake card for every photo

Your original photo is never edited or cropped. It sits in a cream Polaroid frame with a handwritten caption, the way you'd keep a print.

  • 3D flip between photo and note
  • A date stamp on every back
  • Smart captions for photos you haven't written on yet

in your own hand

Write the way you used to

Handwrite with your finger or Apple Pencil on ruled paper, in your choice of ink. Want to type instead? Switch on any card.

  • Finger or Apple Pencil
  • Typing, if you'd rather
  • Ink colors on cream paper

ask, don't scroll

Search your own handwriting

Every note you write is transcribed on your iPhone and becomes searchable. Natural-language search finds the moment even if you don't remember the exact words.

  • Handwriting transcription on your iPhone
  • Search by note, description, category or place
  • Smart search that understands what you mean

a story, not a camera roll

Albums that tell themselves

Group cards into albums and browse them by time. On-device AI can narrate an album's story back to you. When you want to share one, publish it as a web link that anyone can open and flip through, no app needed.

  • Albums sorted by time
  • Album stories written by on-device AI
  • Share an album as a link, take it down whenever you want

Private by design

Your photos and handwriting are yours. PhotoBack has no account and no server of its own.

📱

AI runs on your iPhone

Captions, mood tags, album stories and search all run on your device. Nothing is sent to an AI server.

☁️

Your own iCloud

If you turn on sync, cards sync through your private iCloud account. We can't see them.

🔑

No account

No sign-up, no login, no email. Open the app and start writing.

🚫

No ads, no ad tracking

We collect only anonymous crash reports and basic usage events. Never your photos, notes or handwriting.
